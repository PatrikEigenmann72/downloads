# arcade

If you grew up in the 1980s, you know exactly what it was: a few quarters in your pocket,
walking to the back of that restaurant on the corner of your street, where they had this one
game machine - a cabinet with a screen built into it, lights blinking, daring you to come closer.
You'd feed it quarter after quarter, and for a few hours you could shoot down enemies in 1942,
trade punches as a karate champion, or dive into a fantasy world as Mario.

arcade is my way of bringing those childhood experiences back to life. But recreating that magic
isn't just about redrawing the sprites and matching the sound effects - the real challenge is
matching the difficulty. Those old cabinets were built to eat your quarters: tight timing,
unforgiving hitboxes, and just enough progress to make you want to feed it one more coin. My
arcade projects chase that same feeling - fair, but merciless, exactly how I remember it.

Go ahead, press start on whichever game pulls you back to that corner restaurant - each program
here ships with both a binary and its source code. Right now, arcade builds are macOS only, but
Windows support is coming - I'm working hard to get it there.
