# maze-chomp

Eat every dot in the maze while four ghosts hunt you down - a port of the classic dot-eating arcade
game, with the original's ghost personalities (Blinky chases, Pinky ambushes, Inky pincers, Clyde
loses interest up close), scatter/chase phases, scoring and bonus fruit table.

- **Bonus fruit** twice per maze, a different one every level or two (cherry 100 up to key 5000).
  The orange makes you invincible for 8 seconds, the apple starts an 8-second ghost hunt.
- **Leaderboard** of the top 100 scores, under a player name you set once.
- **Music** on the title page and during a ghost hunt.
- **Help** in the game itself: Fn+F1 on the title page, after GAME OVER or while paused.

Controls: arrows or WASD steer, P pauses, M toggles sound, ESC quits. SPACE starts a game, D shows
the leaderboard, N sets your player name.

`maze-chomp --version` prints the version and exits without opening the game.
