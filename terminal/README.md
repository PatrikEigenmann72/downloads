# terminal

In the beginning of computing, there was no graphical interface to interact with the computer.
What we had was the so-called black void - the Command Line Interface, or CLI for short. No
matter if you were staring at a big IBM terminal connected to a mainframe, tapping away at a
Personal Computer with MS-DOS on it, or working a UNIX machine - the concept was more or less
the same: a black (or blue, or green) void with a blinking cursor, and you standing or sitting
there thinking, what the heck do I do with this thing. Yep, that's the CLI.

Back when I was a kid, I quickly understood what I could do with that cursor. Of course I had a
learning curve, but I started writing BASIC programs at eight years old. The commands you type at
that blinking cursor are CLI tools, and replicating some of these old-school commands that aren't
around anymore is a genuinely fun way to spend spare time. But I digress.

Go ahead, explore my CLI tools - each program ships with both a binary and its source code. Feel
free to grab whichever one catches your eye.
